Sources of the recorded sound effects in this folder (besides the Kenney
"Impact Sounds" files covered by LICENSE-kenney.txt).

Every source below is CC0 1.0 / public domain. No attribution is required.
Credits are optional; the authors would appreciate them:
  "Additional sounds: Joseph SARDIN - BigSoundBank.com"
  "Sounds: Nicholas A. Judy - The Designer's Choice"

All files were trimmed, faded, normalized and converted to mono 44.1 kHz MP3.
Files marked "layered" mix several of the listed recordings together.
Times in [brackets] are the excerpt taken from the original file.
